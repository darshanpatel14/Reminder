Why HourPulse?

Direct iPhone Installation

Method 1 • Instant (0 Tools)

Add to Home Screen (PWA)

1. Ensure your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi network as this Mac.

2. Open Safari on your iPhone and go to:
http://192.168.1.3:3000

3. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow) at the bottom of Safari.

4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".

5. Tap Add in the top right. HourPulse will appear as a standalone app on your iPhone!

Method 2 • Native .IPA Sideloading

Direct .IPA via Sideloadly / AltStore

1. The direct executable package is located at:
HourPulse.ipa

2. Download free Sideloadly (sideloadly.io) on Mac.

3. Plug your iPhone into your Mac via USB.

4. Drag HourPulse.ipa into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, and click Start.

5. On your iPhone, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID profile.

Method 3 • Xcode 1-Click Run

Xcode Direct USB Deploy

1. Open HourPulse-iOS/HourPulse.xcodeproj in Xcode.

2. Select your connected iPhone from the run targets.

3. Click the Play / Run button (Cmd+R).
